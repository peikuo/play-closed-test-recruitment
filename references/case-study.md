# Anonymized weather-app recruitment case

This is a historical recruitment snapshot, not a live count. Project/app names are generalized. It excludes emails, private messages, device IDs, personal paths and private Console URLs.

## Result and limits

After refresh, Console marked the at-least-twelve opt-in threshold complete without displaying an exact count. Continuous fourteen-day eligibility and production approval were not complete. The project's Google group had twelve members including the owner; this cannot identify each Console participant.

Fifteen joined groups consisted of eleven other app test groups, our own group and three unrelated old groups. Fifteen groups are not fifteen reciprocal testers. Old groups were not left without user instruction.

The workflow searched Google Groups and Reddit, checked conditions, deduplicated authors, sent authorized individual invitations, checked comments and chats, matched new members, joined ordinary test groups, checked Play eligibility, installed/launched free apps on a user-connected Android phone, saved screenshots, tried limited flows, delivered truthful feedback, tracked link/device/upload issues and maintained scheduled checks. It did not verify a complete fourteen-day period or production approval.

## Division of work

| Area | AI | Human or platform |
|---|---|---|
| Recruitment | Screen posts, deduplicate, draft/send authorized invitations | User chooses goal, budget, channels and exchange intent |
| Eligibility | Check group/opt-in/store and errors | User supplies login; platform determines region/device access |
| Device trial | Authorized installation, launch, screenshot, limited flow | User connects/unlocks, handles sensitive steps, sustained use and complex experience |
| Feedback | Record steps/issues and deliver real evidence | User adds subjective assessment; developer fixes and retests |
| Monitoring | Short baseline, new responses, evidence levels | User sets interval/cost; testers decide actual participation |
| Review | Assemble facts about use, feedback and improvements | User confirms submission; Google decides eligibility/approval |

AI reduces search, coordination and recordkeeping work. It cannot manufacture genuine participants, replace sustained human use or guarantee approval.

## Lessons and corrections

1. Broad outreach received relatively few replies; continue finding suitable developers and provide specific evidence of our own trials. Accurate conversion rates were unavailable because records/time windows differed and cross-channel identities could overlap.
2. Contact each author once and choose one app even if they post several. A user's proof-before-reciprocity exception remains individual; never turn it into nationality-based screening.
3. A public installation reply was initially misattributed from another user. Match our comment identity and nesting before counting a response.
4. Email-like member names were matched with public author searches. Do not store emails; request a display name if unmatched.
5. A user pointed out a private-chat response missed by public monitoring. Later evidence supported initial weather-app use. Keep channel coverage explicit.
6. A Sudoku app was verified installed/opened after device reconnection; its screenshot and launch observations were delivered in Reddit chat. Earlier feedback links prevented duplicate claims that feedback was missing.
7. A free color-guessing game opened and supported board/color interaction but used Polish. Ask about English and testing links. Failed Google Groups image upload led to verified text feedback and a request for an alternate screenshot destination; image delivery remained pending.
8. An older Play view showed ten before refresh marked the threshold complete. Preserve the correction and report at least twelve, not a guessed exact number.
9. Unchanged frequent checks consumed resources. The user changed forty-minute checks to hourly, then two-hourly. Frequency is configurable; access timeout means unknown, not no new candidates.

## Next stage

Maintain genuine continuous participation, collect concrete feedback, fix and retest issues, and use current Console eligibility to prepare production-access materials. Recruitment success is not approval.
